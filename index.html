Hold up fingers on either hand or both – the total number picks the chord (count the thumb too). Change the number to change the chord; lower your hands to stop. Try the Let It Be progression: 1 → 5 → 6 → 4 (I · V · vi · IV), e.g. one finger, then a full hand, then a full hand + one, then four.
